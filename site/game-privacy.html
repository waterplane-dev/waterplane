Privacy: Waterplane Game

Applies to the Waterplane Game Android app (dev.waterplane.game). Effective 16 October 2026. The waterplane app has its own page.

We collect nothing. The game has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising, and it asks for no permissions at all.

What the phone stores

In the game's own storage on your phone: your best score and stars for each difficulty, the difficulty you last picked, and whether sound is muted. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes all of it. If Android backup is on for your device, Android may include these settings in your device backup, under your backup provider's terms.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
