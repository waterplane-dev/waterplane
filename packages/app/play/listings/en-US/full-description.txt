waterplane is a phone client for Claude Code running on your own desktop. The agent writes the code; the app gives you the steering: live sessions across your repos, diffs and commits, the screenshots and builds it makes, a queue and a schedule, and what every turn costs.

It drives the real Claude Code command-line tool in a terminal on your desktop, not a copy of it, so slash commands, plan prompts and the context meter work as they do at your desk.

What you need:
• A desktop (macOS, Linux, or Windows with WSL2) with Claude Code and the free, open-source waterplane daemon installed
• A way for the phone to reach it: Tailscale from anywhere, or a desktop password on the same Wi-Fi

Without a desktop, only the demo works. Setup: https://github.com/waterplane-dev/waterplane

What you can do:
• Run sessions in many repos at once, as the live terminal or in a clean reader
• Get a notification when a session needs you or finishes; its questions and plan prompts come up as buttons
• Write prompts offline; they are delivered when the link is back
• Queue prompts behind a busy session, or schedule them for later or for after a usage limit
• Review diffs, commit and push
• See screenshots and builds in a gallery, mark one up and send it back with your prompt
• Send photos and files to the agent, and download what it builds
• Install the Android apps you build on your desktop (Android asks you before every install)
• See usage, context and cost per turn, and the health of each repo

There is no waterplane server and no account: the app connects only to your own desktop. Tap Try the demo on the first screen to explore with sample data; nothing connects and nothing runs.
